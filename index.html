Residential Compliance Paths in Climate Zone 6

Mandatory Path Selection

Under NYStretch-2020 Section R401.2, every residential building shall demonstrate compliance using one of the four recognized design pathways:

1. Prescriptive Path R401 – R404
Direct component verification against tabular limits & mandatory rules.
  • Adhere to prescriptive R-values (Table R402.1.2) or assembly U-factors (Table R402.1.4).
  • Continuous insulation strictly required on wood-framed walls (R-20+5ci or R-13+10ci).
  • Ducts 100% inside conditioned thermal envelope (R403.3).
  • Balanced whole-house HRV or ERV ventilation (R403.6.2).
  • Drain Water Heat Recovery (DWHR) and hot water pipe volume limits (R403.5).
2. Energy Rating Index (ERI) Section R406
Performance simulation index path (RESNET HERS calculation).
  • Maximum ERI Score: 50 for Climate Zone 6 (Table R406.4).
  • Must satisfy all mandatory checklist provisions of Sections R401 through R404.
  • Envelope Backstop: When on-site renewable generation is credited, the thermal envelope cannot be downgraded below 2015 IECC Table R402.1.2/R402.1.4 levels.
3. Simulated Performance Section R405
Annual energy cost budget trade-off analysis (R-2, R-3, R-4).
  • Stringency requirement: Proposed building energy cost must be ≤ 80% of standard reference design (20% annual energy cost reduction).
  • Must satisfy all mandatory envelope air tightness and mechanical provisions.
4. Passive House Path Section R408
Streamlined compliance via PHIUS+ or PHI certified buildings.
  • Certified under PHIUS+ 2015 (space heat demand ≤ 9 kBTU/ft²/yr, air leakage ≤ 0.05 CFM50/ft²) or PHI (demand ≤ 9.5 kBTU/ft²/yr, ACH50 ≤ 1.0).
  • Deemed to satisfy energy code requirements upon verified CPHC submittal.

IBC §1202.3 • IRC §R806.5 & §R702.7 • ASHRAE Fundamentals

Condensation Control & Vapor Retarder Architecture

Cold Climate Hygrothermal Physics
The Building Science of Condensation Control in CZ6: In cold winter conditions, warm humid indoor air (68°F–72°F, 30–45% RH, dew point 40°F–50°F) naturally migrates outward by vapor diffusion and air exfiltration. When moist air encounters surfaces colder than its dew point—most critically the exterior wood roof deck or wall sheathing—it condenses into liquid water and frost. Both the International Residential Code (IRC) and International Building Code (IBC) mandate thermal ratio rules to keep potential condensing planes above dew point.
Unvented Roofs: Over-Deck Rigid Board IRC R806.5.1.1
Continuous rigid insulation installed entirely on the exterior side of roof deck.

When rigid insulation boards (polyisocyanurate, extruded polystyrene XPS, or rigid stone wool) are placed entirely above the structural roof sheathing:

  • Minimum Air-Impermeable R-Value: In Climate Zone 6, minimum R-25 continuous rigid insulation must be placed above the roof sheathing (Table R806.5) when paired with air-permeable cavity batts below.
  • Total Assembly Integration: Under NYStretch CZ6 (target R-49 ceiling), the assembly can be configured as R-25 over-deck + R-24 cavity batts, or 100% over-deck rigid insulation (R-49 continuous).
  • Dew Point Control: By placing at least R-25 outside the structural wood deck, the wood sheathing is kept warm and isolated from the condensing zone, eliminating winter condensation risk.
  • Sheathing Joint Taping: The roof sheathing must be taped and sealed to serve as a continuous air barrier beneath the rigid insulation.
Unvented Roofs: Underside Closed-Cell SPF IRC R806.5.1.2
Direct-to-deck closed-cell spray polyurethane foam (ccSPF).

Medium-density 2.0 lb/cu.ft closed-cell spray polyurethane foam applied directly to the underside of the wood roof deck:

  • Minimum R-25 ccSPF (Hybrid / Flash-and-Batt): In CZ6, minimum R-25 closed-cell spray foam (~3.8 to 4.0 inches at R-6.5/in) must be sprayed in direct contact with the underside of roof sheathing before fibrous air-permeable insulation (fiberglass/mineral wool batts) can be added below.
  • Dual Function Air Barrier + Vapor Retarder: Closed-cell foam at ≥ 1.5 inches achieves ≤ 1.0 perm, qualifying as an impermeable air barrier and Class II vapor retarder that stops humid indoor air from reaching the cold deck.
  • Full-Depth ccSPF: If using spray foam for the entire assembly, apply full R-49 depth (~7.5 inches of ccSPF).
  • Thermal Barrier: Must be separated from dwelling space by an approved thermal barrier (1/2" gypsum board per IRC R316.4) or code-approved intumescent coating.

Wall Vapor Retarders: IRC §R702.7 & CZ6 Table R702.7.1
Class I, II, and III vapor retarder requirements in Climate Zone 6.

In Climate Zone 6, interior vapor retarders are regulated by IRC Section R702.7 to prevent moisture diffusion into wall cavities:

  • Class I Vapor Retarder (≤ 0.1 perm): Sheet polyethylene or unperforated foil. Caution: Polyethylene traps moisture inward during summer cooling periods if homes are air conditioned.
  • Class II Vapor Retarder (0.1 – 1.0 perm): Kraft paper facing or polyamide "smart" vapor retarders (such as Intello or MemBrain) that vary perm rating based on relative humidity.
  • Class III Vapor Retarder (1.0 – 10 perm): Latex paint on gypsum board. Permitted in CZ6 only when continuous exterior insulation meets Table R702.7.1:
    • 2x4 framing: Minimum R-7.5 continuous exterior insulation.
    • 2x6 framing: Minimum R-11.25 continuous exterior insulation.
  • NYStretch CZ6 Synergy: Specifying R-13 + 10ci in 2x4 walls satisfies the R-7.5 exterior threshold, safely enabling Class III interior latex paint and eliminating polyethylene vapor barriers!
Vented Attics: Ventilation & Baffles IRC §R806.1 – §R806.3
Natural attic cross-ventilation and eave clearance rules.

Where attics are vented to the exterior rather than conditioned into the thermal envelope:

  • Ventilation Area Ratio: 1:150 net free ventilating area (NFVA) of the attic space, reducible to 1:300 when balanced 40–50% at ridge/upper vents and 50–60% at soffits with a Class I/II ceiling vapor retarder.
  • Mandatory Eave Baffles (R806.3): Rigid air-blocking baffles must be installed at every rafter bay over exterior wall top plates.
  • 1-Inch Clear Airspace: Baffles must maintain not less than a 1-inch continuous clear airspace between the top of the insulation and the underside of the roof sheathing from soffit to ridge.
  • Wind Washing Prevention: Baffles prevent high-velocity outdoor wind from blowing through fibrous attic insulation and degrading the ceiling thermal resistance at perimeter plates.

NYStretch Sections 3.6, 3.7, 3.11, 3.12 • R403.3 & R403.6

Mechanical, Duct Location & Balanced Ventilation

Mandatory in CZ6
HRV / ERV Mandatory R403.6.2
Every dwelling unit in CZ6 must have balanced heat/energy recovery.

In new construction, every dwelling unit must be served by a balanced Heat Recovery Ventilator (HRV) or Energy Recovery Ventilator (ERV) sized for conditioned floor area and design occupancy.

Strict CZ6 Distinction: The central furnace blower outdoor air intake exception (CFIS) is only permitted in Climate Zone 4. In Climate Zone 6, a true balanced HRV or ERV is mandatory.
Ducts in Conditioned Space R403.3
Zero thermal penalty inside unconditioned attics.

All duct systems and air handling units in new buildings and additions shall be located completely inside conditioned space per Sections R403.3.7(1) and (2).

  • Eliminates seasonal convective duct leakage losses in vented attics.
  • Permitted inside conditioned basements, drop soffits, open-web floor trusses, or unvented conditioned attics.
ACCA Manual D & Verification R403.3.8 & R403.6.3
Engineered duct airflow sizing and independent flow testing.

Mandatory Duct Sizing: All air distribution systems must be engineered per ACCA Manual D based on ACCA Manual J (heating/cooling loads) and Manual S (equipment selection).

Field Flow Testing: Mechanical ventilation airflow rates must be field-verified with a flow hood or flow grid by an approved agency per ANSI/ACCA 9QIvp-2016.

Hot Water Recirculation Controls R403.5.5.4

Where continuous or demand hot water circulation loops are employed:

  • Storage Volume Ceiling: Maximum allowable storage between the recirculation loop tee and any fixture is 0.5 gallons (1.9 liters).
  • Demand Activation: Recirculation systems must be triggered by an occupant push-button switch or occupancy sensor installed in each bathroom located beyond a 0.5-gallon volume from the water heater.
  • Continuous gravity circulation loops and unmanaged mechanical timers are strictly prohibited.

NYStretch Sections 3.13 – 3.14 • R404.1 & R404.2

Electrical, High-Efficacy Lighting, Solar-Ready & EVSE

Mandatory
High-Efficacy Lighting R404.1

≥ 90% of permanently installed fixtures must utilize lamps or luminaires satisfying one of:

  • Lamps with efficacy ≥ 65 lumens per watt, OR
  • Total luminaire efficacy ≥ 45 lumens per watt.
  • Fuel Gas Lighting (R404.1.1): Permanently installed fuel-gas lighting shall NOT have continuously burning pilot lights.
Solar-Ready Zone R404.2 #1

Mandatory for new detached one- and two-family dwellings and townhouses where conditioned space exceeds 1,400 sq ft (130 m²).

  • Compliant with the provisions of Appendix RA.
  • Designated unshaded solar roof zone free from vents, flues, and roof obstructions.
  • Electrical main service panel must provide reserved space for a future dual-pole solar breaker.
EV Charging Infrastructure R404.2 #2

Mandatory electrical vehicle supply equipment infrastructure for on-site parking spaces:

  • Single & 2-Family & Townhouses: Provide a dedicated 208/240V 40-amp outlet for each dwelling unit OR panel capacity + capped conduit terminating adjacent to parking.
  • Common Parking Areas: Provide 208/240V 40-amp outlets (or panel capacity + conduit) for 5% of total spaces, min 1 space.
NYStretch Section 3.16 • Section R408

Passive House Compliance Option (PHIUS+ & PHI)

Alternative Compliance Pathway

NYStretch adds Section R408, providing an explicit alternative compliance avenue for certified Passive House buildings. Qualifying projects satisfy envelope, mechanical, and energy requirements when verified under either recognized standard:

PHIUS+ (Passive House Institute US) R408.1.1
PHIUS+ 2015: Passive Building Standard – North America
  • Specific Space Heat & Cooling Demand: Less than or equal to 9.0 kBTU/ft²/year (sensible only cooling).
  • Blower Door Air Leakage: Must exhibit no more than 0.05 CFM50/ft² or 0.08 CFM75/ft².
  • Consultant Role: Modeled and field-verified by a Certified Passive House Consultant (CPHC).
PHI (Passive House Institute) R408.1.2
PHI 2016: Low Energy Building Standard, Version 9f
  • Specific Heating & Cooling Demand: Less than or equal to 9.5 kBTU/ft²/year (sensible only cooling).
  • Blower Door Air Leakage: Infiltration rate of no more than 1.0 ACH at 50 Pascals (1.0 ACH50).
  • Consultant Role: Modeled and field-verified by a Certified Passive House Consultant (CPHC).
