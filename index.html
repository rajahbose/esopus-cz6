NYStretch Section 3.1 • R401.2

Residential Compliance Paths in Climate Zone 6

Mandatory Selection

Under NYStretch-2020 Section R401.2, projects shall achieve code approval by qualifying under one of the four recognized compliance pathways:

1. Prescriptive Path R401 – R404

Direct component-by-component adherence to tables & mandatory rules.

  • Must comply with all prescriptive insulation values in Table R402.1.2 or assembly U-factors in Table R402.1.4.
  • Ducts strictly in conditioned space (R403.3).
  • Mandatory balanced HRV/ERV for every dwelling (R403.6.2).
  • Mandatory Drain Water Heat Recovery and pipe length/volume controls (R403.5.5).
2. Energy Rating Index (ERI) R406

Performance simulation index path (e.g., RESNET HERS).

  • Maximum ERI Score: 50 for Climate Zone 6 (Table R406.4).
  • Must meet all mandatory requirements of Sections R401 through R404.
  • Renewable Energy Backstop: When on-site renewable energy is credited, the thermal envelope must not be compromised below 2015 IECC Table R402.1.2/R402.1.4 levels.
3. Simulated Performance R405 (R-2, R-3, R-4)

Whole-building annual energy cost budget trade-off approach.

  • Applies to Group R-2, Group R-3, and Group R-4 buildings.
  • Stringency requirement: Proposed building energy cost must be ≤ 80% of standard reference design (a mandatory 20% cost reduction over base code!).
  • Must satisfy all provisions of Sections R401 through R404 labeled "Mandatory".
4. Passive House Path R408 (New Option)

Streamlined compliance via PHIUS+ or PHI certification.

  • Certified under PHIUS+ 2015 (demand ≤ 9 kBTU/ft²/yr, air leakage ≤ 0.05 CFM50/ft²) or PHI (demand ≤ 9.5 kBTU/ft²/yr, ACH50 ≤ 1.0).
  • Requires CPHC (Certified Passive House Consultant) pre-construction plans and verified final occupancy report.
  • Automatically satisfies envelope and energy requirements.

Ceilings Without Attic Spaces R402.2.2

Where Table R402.1.2 requires insulation > R-38 (CZ6 requires R-49) and structural roof/ceiling design does not allow adequate clearance:

  • Minimum allowed insulation: R-38.
  • Must extend over the exterior wall top plate without compression.
  • Strict Area Cap: Limited to 500 sq ft (46 m²) or 20% of total insulated ceiling area, whichever is less.
  • Does NOT apply to the U-factor or Total UA trade-off alternative methods.
Mandatory Open-Wall Air Barrier & Insulation Inspection R402.4.1.1

Strict visual quality installation protocol before drywall enclosure:

  • Third-party verification: An approved inspection agency must verify all thermal envelope assemblies.
  • Cavity fill: Insulation must uniformly fill cavities side-to-side and top-to-bottom without substantial voids or bundling.
  • Wiring & plumbing: Batt insulation must be split around wiring and piping.
  • 2% Compression Rule: No more than 2 percent of the total insulated area may be compressed below the rated thickness.
NYStretch Sections 3.6, 3.7, 3.11, 3.12 • R403.3 & R403.6

Mechanical, Ductwork & Ventilation Requirements

Mandatory in CZ6
HRV / ERV Mandatory R403.6.2

Every dwelling unit in CZ6 must have heat or energy recovery.

In new construction, every dwelling unit must be served by a balanced Heat Recovery Ventilator (HRV) or Energy Recovery Ventilator (ERV) sized for conditioned floor area and occupancy.

Note on CZ4 Exception: The exception permitting central furnace blower outdoor air intake is only allowed in Climate Zone 4. In Climate Zone 6, a true balanced HRV or ERV is non-negotiable!
Ducts in Conditioned Space R403.3

Zero thermal penalty inside unconditioned attics.

The entire duct system and air handlers in new buildings and additions shall be located completely inside conditioned space in accordance with Sections R403.3.7(1) and (2).

  • Eliminates massive seasonal duct leakage losses in vented attics.
  • Permitted inside conditioned basements, drop ceilings, open web floor trusses, or unvented conditioned attics.
ACCA Manual D & Verification R403.3.8 & R403.6.3

Engineered airflow sizing and independent flow-hood testing.

Mandatory Duct Sizing: All duct distribution systems must be engineered per ACCA Manual D based on ACCA Manual J (heating/cooling loads) and Manual S (equipment selection).

Field Flow Testing: Mechanical ventilation airflows must be tested and verified with a flow hood or flow grid by an approved agency in accordance with ANSI/ACCA 9QIvp-2016.

Hot Water Recirculation Limits R403.5.5.4

Where continuous or demand hot water circulation loops are employed:

  • Storage Volume Ceiling: Maximum allowable storage between the recirculation loop tee and any fixture is 0.5 gallons (1.9 liters).
  • Demand Activation: Recirculation systems must be triggered by an occupant-controlled push-button switch or occupancy sensor installed in each bathroom located beyond a 0.5-gallon volume from the water heater.
  • Continuous gravity circulation loops or uncontrolled timers are prohibited.

NYStretch Sections 3.13 – 3.14 • R404.1 & R404.2

Electrical, High-Efficacy Lighting, Solar-Ready & EVSE

Mandatory
High-Efficacy Lighting R404.1

≥ 90% of permanently installed fixtures must utilize lamps or luminaires satisfying one of:

  • Lamps with efficacy ≥ 65 lumens per watt, OR
  • Total luminaire efficacy ≥ 45 lumens per watt.
  • Fuel Gas Lighting (R404.1.1): Permanently installed fuel-gas lighting shall NOT have continuously burning pilot lights.
Solar-Ready Zone R404.2 #1

Mandatory for new detached one- and two-family dwellings and townhouses where the conditioned space is greater than 1,400 sq ft (130 m²).

  • Must comply with the provisions of Appendix RA.
  • Requires a designated solar zone on roof/site free from vents, pipes, and obstructions oriented south for future PV/solar thermal.
  • Electrical panel must provide reserved space for dual-pole solar breakers.
EV Charging Capable R404.2 #2

Mandatory infrastructure for on-site parking spaces:

  • Single & 2-Family & Townhouses: Provide a dedicated 208/240V 40-amp outlet for each dwelling unit OR panel capacity + capped conduit terminating adjacent to parking.
  • Common Parking Areas: Provide 208/240V 40-amp outlets (or panel capacity + conduit) for 5% of total spaces, min 1 space.
NYStretch Section 3.16 • Section R408

Passive House Compliance Option (PHIUS+ & PHI)

Deemed to Satisfy

NYStretch adds Section R408, providing an explicit alternative compliance avenue for certified Passive House buildings. Qualifying projects satisfy envelope, mechanical, and energy requirements when verified under either recognized standard:

PHIUS+ (Passive House Institute US) R408.1.1

PHIUS+ 2015: Passive Building Standard – North America

  • Specific Space Heat & Cooling Demand: Less than or equal to 9.0 kBTU/ft²/year (sensible only cooling).
  • Blower Door Air Leakage: Must exhibit no more than 0.05 CFM50/ft² or 0.08 CFM75/ft².
  • Consultant Role: Modeled and field-verified by a Certified Passive House Consultant (CPHC).
PHI (Passive House Institute) R408.1.2

PHI 2016: Low Energy Building Standard, Version 9f

  • Specific Heating & Cooling Demand: Less than or equal to 9.5 kBTU/ft²/year (sensible only cooling).
  • Blower Door Air Leakage: Infiltration rate of no more than 1.0 ACH at 50 Pascals (1.0 ACH50).
  • Consultant Role: Modeled and field-verified by a Certified Passive House Consultant (CPHC).
Required Submittal & Permitting Documentation (R408.2)
Phase 1: Prior to Building Permit Issuance

1. Detailed list of compliance features.
2. Formal signed statement certifying estimated Specific Space Heat Demand is "based on plans".

Phase 2: Prior to Certificate of Occupancy

Copy of official final report on approved form documenting completed building achieves verified specific space heat demand ≤ 9 kBTU/ft²/yr (PHIUS) or ≤ 9.5 kBTU/ft²/yr (PHI) verified by CPHC.
