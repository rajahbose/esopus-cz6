# NYStretch Energy Code 2020 – Climate Zone 6 Guide

Interactive technical guide and reference for the **NYStretch Energy Code 2020** (an overlay of the 2018 International Energy Conservation Code) specifically tailored for **Residential Buildings in Climate Zone 6** (e.g., Town of Esopus, Ulster County, and upstate New York cold climate jurisdictions).

## 📄 Contents

- `index.html` - Full interactive single-page guide, featuring:
  - **Envelope Thermal Performance**: Prescriptive R-values (Table R402.1.2) and Assembly U-factors (Table R402.1.4).
  - **Wood-Framed Wall Continuous Insulation Mandate**: Breakdown of R-20+5 or R-13+10 requirement.
  - **Condensation Control & Vapor Retarder Architecture**: IBC §1202.3 and IRC §R806.5 / §R702.7 rules, unvented roof air-impermeable R-25 minimum thresholds (over-deck rigid vs. underside closed-cell SPF vs. ocSPF), wall vapor permeance, and an interactive condensation ratio checker.
  - **Mechanical Systems**: Mandatory ducts in conditioned space and balanced HRV/ERV requirements (R403.6.2).
  - **Domestic Hot Water (DHW)**: Mandatory Drain Water Heat Recovery (DWHR) and maximum pipe length/volume tables (Table R403.5.5.1).
  - **Electrical & Clean Energy**: High-efficacy lighting (&ge;90%), Solar-Ready Zones (Appendix RA), and EV charging readiness.
  - **Passive House Alternative**: PHIUS+ and PHI compliance parameters (Section R408).
  - **Interactive Tools**: Built-in DHW Pipe Run Verifier, Condensation Ratio Checker, and Plan Review Checklist.
  - **Commercial Group R Reference**: Requirements for multi-family residential > 3 stories.
- `NYStretch-Energy-Code-2020.pdf` - The complete source code document published by NYSERDA (Version 1.0, July 2019).
